Our Story

Turning a environmental burden into a sustainable opportunity.

The Dark Side of Coffee

Behind every cup of coffee lies a hidden waste problem. Most used coffee grounds end up in landfills, where they decompose anaerobically and release methane gas—contributing significantly to global warming.

In Indonesia alone, the massive coffee culture generates thousands of tons of waste daily. We saw this not as a problem, but as an untapped resource.

The KOPAS Way

KOPAS was born from a simple question: "How can we give coffee a second life?"

We partner with local cafes and hotels to collect their "ampas kopi" (coffee waste). Through our specialized process, we dehydrate, refine, and compress this waste into beautiful, functional products.

  • Collection: Zero-emissions waste management partnerships.
  • Upcycling: Patented compression techniques for durability.
  • Impact: Creating a truly circular loop for the hospitality industry.
